## PRICING STRATEGIES

### Dynamic Pricing Considerations:

1. **Peak Hours (Weekends, Evenings):**
   - Consider 10-15% premium
   - Or offer discounts for off-peak times (weekday mornings)

2. **Weather-Based:**
   - Pre-storm discounts to encourage bookings
   - Post-storm premium (high demand)

3. **Loyalty Rewards:**
   - 5th wash free (for non-members)
   - Referral bonus: $20 credit
   - Birthday month: 15% off

4. **First-Time Customer:**
   - 20% off first service
   - Free upgrade (Basic → Premium)

5. **Holiday Specials:**
   - Valentine's Day: Couple's package
   - Mother's/Father's Day: Gift certificates
   - End of Year: Full detail special

## PROFITABILITY CALCULATOR

### Cost Per Service (Example - Full Service Sedan)

**Revenue:** $45

**Costs:**
- Labor (30 min @ $20/hr): $10
- Supplies (soap, wax, water): $3

**Gross Profit:** $25
**Profit Margin:** 55%

### Target Margins by Service Type:
- Basic Services: 40-50%
- Premium Services: 50-60%
- Detailing Services: 55-65%
- Memberships: 60-70% (long-term value)

## IMPLEMENTATION TIPS

1. **Start Conservative:** 
   - Begin with mid-range pricing
   - Test market response
   - Adjust based on demand

2. **Value-Add Services:**
   - Free vacuum with any wash
   - Complimentary air freshener
   - Waiting area amenities

3. **Clear Communication:**
   - Display prices prominently
   - Explain what's included
   - Offer service comparison charts

4. **Online Booking:**
   - Website with clear pricing
   - Online booking system
   - Digital payment options

5. **Seasonal Adjustments:**
   - Review pricing quarterly
   - Adjust for inflation and costs
   - Special promotions during slow periods

6. **Track Everything:**
   - Most popular services
   - Average transaction value
   - Customer preferences
   - Peak times
