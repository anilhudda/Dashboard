- Running totals

  2. Equipment & Tools (washing equipment, vacuums)
  3. Supplies & Consumables (chemicals, towels)

  5. Marketing & Advertising

  - Other Revenue

Your command center with:

### MONTHLY RECONCILIATION SHEET
End-of-month closing process:

## 👥 Partner Preparation

### Before Setup, Gather:
1. **All 4 partner names** (as they should appear in system)
2. **Initial investment amounts** from each partner
3. **Any expenses already incurred** (with receipts)
4. **Any revenue already collected**
5. **Business start date**
6. **Agreement on ownership percentages** (or will auto-calculate based on contributions)

### Roles to Assign:
- **System Administrator**: Sets up and maintains the sheet
- **Data Entry**: Records daily transactions (can be multiple people)
- **Reviewer**: Weekly verification of entries
- **Reconciler**: Monthly closing process
- **Backup Person**: Knows the system in case primary person unavailable

## 💡 Success Tips

### DO:
✅ Enter transactions daily (don't let them pile up)  
✅ Keep receipts organized and referenced  
✅ Review dashboard weekly  
✅ Complete monthly reconciliation on time  
✅ Communicate with all partners regularly  
✅ Backup important data  
✅ Use dropdowns (data validation) for consistency  

### DON'T:
❌ Skip entering transactions  
❌ Delete or rename sheets without updating formulas  
❌ Ignore error messages (#REF!, #NAME?)  
❌ Wait until month-end to enter all data  
❌ Forget to save receipts  
❌ Make major changes without partner agreement

### Common Issues

**Problem**: Formulas showing #REF!  
**Solution**: Sheet name doesn't match formula reference exactly

**Problem**: Dropdown not working  
**Solution**: Re-create data validation, check spelling

**Problem**: Chart not updating  
**Solution**: Edit chart, verify data range includes all data
